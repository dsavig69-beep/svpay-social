One shop paid about $2,250 to lease a card machine that cost about $400 new, per an FTC complaint against Riverside Payments.

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says Riverside's quote sheets at times promised to cut up to 60% of a shop's card fees, but shops that switched typically paid more. Swipe for the fine print and 4 things to get in writing before you sign with any processor, SVPay included.

Riverside agreed to a proposed $600,000 settlement with the FTC and Washington state. If a judge approves it, customers get 60 days to end the processing contract with no termination fee.
