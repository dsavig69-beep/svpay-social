One small business paid about $2,250 to lease a card machine that cost around $400 new, the FTC says. Leases like it couldn't be canceled.

The FTC and Washington state sued the processor, Riverside Payments, on Nov 2. Riverside promised shops 40% to 60% off their card fees, and the FTC says shops that switched typically paid more. Riverside denies it.
