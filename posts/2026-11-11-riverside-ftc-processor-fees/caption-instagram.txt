An FTC complaint says one North Carolina business owner spent about $2,250 leasing a $400 card machine over 4 years. Leases like it couldn't be canceled.

I started SVPay, and my advice is simple: get every term in writing before you sign.

The FTC and Washington state sued the processor, Riverside Payments, on Nov 2. The FTC says Riverside's pitch promised 40% to 60% off card fees, and that shops that switched typically paid more. Riverside denies it.

Swipe for the fine print and four questions to ask.
